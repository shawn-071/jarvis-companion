Built from the reference

JARVIS glasses

A refined concept based on the two new photos: a chunky dark frame, one front-facing HC-SR04 above the bridge, and two side-facing sensors. The ESP32-CAM sits on the viewer’s right lens beneath sensor 03.

  • FrameDark rectangular glasses
  • Ultrasonic layout1 front · 2 sides
  • Haptics2 × vibration motors

The OBJ uses a matching material file. Keep JARVIS_Glasses.mtl beside it when importing into a 3D editor.
